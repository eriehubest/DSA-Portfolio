# DSA Supporting Write-Up

This document is meant to accompany the website and serve as a first introduction before an examiner decides whether to explore the portfolio in more detail.

## Main Version

I am applying through DSA because mathematics and computing have become the two main ways I explore difficult problems. In mathematics, I developed my interest through sustained participation in competitions, where I learned to look for structure carefully, build precise arguments, and remain accurate under pressure. Over several years, this grew into a consistent record across UKMT competitions and the AMC.

In computing, my path has been largely self-taught and independent. Rather than learning only through short exercises, I taught myself by building projects from scratch and gradually taking on more technical complexity. My main work is the portfolio website and its linked project gallery, both of which I designed and developed independently. These projects were built using React, GSAP, Three.js, and related web graphics tools, and they were meant not only to present my work visually, but also to show how I think about structure, interaction, and technical organisation.

The deeper technical project in the gallery is a modular vehicle systems project. In that work, I organised rendering, physics, input handling, camera logic, world construction, resource loading, and timing into separate but connected systems. Building it required more than making individual features work. It required learning how complex parts affect one another, how to keep a project maintainable as it grows, and how to refine behaviour through repeated testing and iteration.

What connects mathematics and computing most strongly for me is structure. Mathematics trained me to care about clarity, invariants, and disciplined reasoning. Computing gave me a way to apply those habits to systems that can be built, tested, and improved. Together, they shaped the way I approach unfamiliar problems: by breaking them down carefully, learning what I need independently, and building towards a coherent result.

Through DSA, I hope to join an environment where I can continue developing both mathematical rigor and technical independence. I am especially interested in opportunities that involve deeper investigation, sustained problem-solving, and the chance to keep learning beyond the syllabus.
